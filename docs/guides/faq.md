# Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about AirShop.

## General

### What is AirShop?

AirShop is a platform for managing quotes, orders, and inventory for manufacturing and fabrication workflows.

### How do I get support?

In the AirShop app, use the **Help** widget in the bottom-right corner to contact AirShop.
